# What happens on each screen

This is the room as it works now. One team, five students and one teacher. The teacher presses **Start** and **Next**. Students fill the fields on their own screen. A field that is not listed here is not on that screen.

The sentence the room is building:

**[who] needs [need], but [obstacle], so [harm], because [insight].**

| Blank | Where it is filled | What to put in it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Power. The Lead Interviewer taps one person. | The person the rest of the activity follows. Not "users" or "people". |
| Need | Statement. Each student edits this. It starts from the interview answer to "What are you trying to get done?" | A human need. Not an app, platform, website, system, dashboard, or feature. |
| Obstacle | Pin, then carried into the statement. | The cause written on the rib that was pinned. |
| Harm | Incident, then carried into the statement. | The winning incident, the moment in the fish head. "What goes wrong" on the power map is only the earlier guess. |
| Insight | Pin, then carried into the statement. | The Why marked as the root cause. |

If you edit Who, Obstacle, Harm, or Insight on the statement, your edit is kept. It is not overwritten.

## Roles

| Role | Fills |
| --- | --- |
| Leader | Keeps the pace. Does not have private fields. The teacher moves the screens. |
| Lead Interviewer | The person the team follows, what goes wrong, the extra interview questions, and the incident. |
| Theme Mapper | Merges duplicate people, places every card on the matrix, moves a cause onto the right rib, and places the pin. |
| Insight Extractor | The Why chain, the root mark, and the two pin labels. |
| Devil's Advocate | Reads for blame and weak sentences. On Choose 3, tags a statement after selecting it. |
| Teacher | Opens the room, plays the films, runs the clock, and presses Start or Next. |

## Opening the room

On the home page the teacher fills:

| Field | How |
| --- | --- |
| Team name | The name of this group. |
| Theme | The problem the room is about. The chocolate example uses "The hidden world behind a chocolate bar". |
| Your name on the radar | The teacher's name. |
| Five student names | One first name each. Names must be different. |
| Role beside each name | Leader, Lead Interviewer, Theme Mapper, Insight Extractor, Devil's Advocate. One person per role. |

**Open room** creates the room and keeps the teacher in that tab. Students join with the four-character code and tap their name.

**Open the chocolate-bar example** skips the empty board and opens a filled cocoa room on the pin screen, with all five seats on one computer. **Open the lunch-waste pilot** opens the older lunch theme at the welcome.

## Welcome

**Screen:** Welcome to C60 · What's the problem.

Nothing for a student to type. Each person checks the role badge. The theme is already on the board.

The teacher sees two films, **The chocolate bar** and **Who is in this, and who do we follow?** Each has a frame that says **Video will play here**, **Play for the class**, and **The class has seen this**. Play both, then press **Start**. Student screens do not start the film. **Read this step** is closed until a student opens it.

## People

**Screen:** Who is touched by this?

Everyone adds people in silence. Other students cannot read the cards until the teacher ends the time.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Stakeholder label | Every student | Type a kind of person, such as "cocoa farmer" or "buying agent". Press **Add my stakeholder**. |

Aim for three cards each. Eight is the limit. Two characters is the shortest label the room accepts. The room rejects an app, a platform, a website, a dashboard, a feature, or a tool as a person. It also rejects blame words such as lazy or stupid.

**Remove** deletes one of your own cards. You cannot edit someone else's card.

The teacher runs the clock, about four minutes, then presses **Next**. That reveals the pile.

## Merge

**Screen:** Clean the pile — merge, don't retype.

The cards are face up. Only the Theme Mapper changes them.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Two cards that mean the same person | Theme Mapper | Drag one card onto the other. They become one stack. The authors stay on the card. |
| Renamed label | Theme Mapper | Tap the stack, type the name it should keep, press **Rename label**. |
| Unmerge | Theme Mapper | Tap a stack, press **Unmerge**, if two different people were joined. |
| Merges done | Theme Mapper | Press this when the pile is clean. The room can also move on when the teacher presses **Next**. |

Everyone else sees the pile and cannot type. A suggested pair, if one is waiting, has **Merge** and **Dismiss** for the Theme Mapper only.

Do not merge an end user with the person who pays, or a farmer with a buyer, just because both are people.

## Power

**Screen:** Who has power? Who cares? Whose problem do we follow?

There is no vote for the person. The Lead Interviewer chooses.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Quadrant | Theme Mapper | Drag each card into one corner, or tap the card and then tap the corner. |
| Who we follow | Lead Interviewer | Tap one person on **Who we follow**, or use the menu **Whose problem we follow**. |
| What goes wrong | Lead Interviewer | A full phrase, at least a short sentence, for that person. Press **Write what goes wrong**. |

The four corners:

| Corner | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| High power, high interest | Work closely |
| High power, low interest | Keep satisfied. Often a sponsor or a blocker. |
| Low power, high interest | Keep involved. Often the person who feels it every day. |
| Low power, low interest | Monitor |

Power means how much they can change what happens. Interest means how much they care.

**What goes wrong** is the early guess. It is not Harm in the final sentence. Harm becomes the incident on the next screen. The room rejects a one-word answer and blame words.

Everyone else watches. They do not move cards and they do not pick the person.

The teacher plays **Talk to them, then pick one moment**, then presses **Next**.

## Incident

**Screen:** Pin one real incident.

The short interview appears for everyone. It is the team asking, and the person you followed answering. It is built from the map. It is not a recording. If that person was marked as a guess, the interview says so.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Extra question | Lead Interviewer only | The box under the interview. Press **Ask**. Up to eight questions. |
| Incident | Lead Interviewer | One fact: a when, a where, and what happened. Press **Add the incident**. |
| Vote | Every student | Press the vote button on an incident card. |

Everyone can read the interview and the answers. No other student has a question box.

Write "At the village buying station, the bag weighed less on the buyer's scale" rather than "farmers are careless". The room rejects blame wording.

The incident with the most votes becomes the fish head when the teacher presses **Next**. If nobody has voted, the first incident is the one that locks.

The teacher plays **The fish, one rib, the root, the pin** before that Next.

## Causes

**Screen:** Map possible causes on the fish.

The head of the fish is the incident. Each person adds causes onto the ribs. This is not the root yet, and it is not a solution.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Bone | Every student | Tap one rib first. |
| Cause | Every student | Why that incident happened, from that angle. Press **Add it to** that rib. |

The six ribs:

| Rib | Write a cause about |
| --- | --- |
| Timing | When it happens, or how long it takes. |
| Communication | What people are told, or not told. |
| Perceived value | Whether it feels worth the effort. |
| Process | The steps people have to follow. |
| Tools | The things people have to use. |
| Environment | The place, or what is around them. |

The cause must be at least a short phrase. The room rejects a solution, such as an app or a platform.

The Theme Mapper can tap a card that landed on the wrong rib, then press **Move here** on the better rib. Other students cannot move a card after it is placed.

The teacher presses **Next** when the fish has causes on it. The room is aiming for causes on at least four ribs.

## Choose

**Screen:** Pick the one bone to follow.

No new text. Read the cause on each rib.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| The rib to follow | Every student | Tap one rib. The button says **We follow this bone** when it is the team's choice. |

The five Whys happen on that rib only. The teacher presses **Next** to open them.

## Why

**Screen:** Dig deeper — ask Why on the rib you followed.

Only the Insight Extractor adds a Why. The answer grows to the left of the card it explains.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Why | Insight Extractor | One reason the selected card happened. |
| Tag | Insight Extractor | Tap Evidence, Assumption, or Need to check before adding. |
| Add Why | Insight Extractor | Press **Add Why 1**, then **Add Why 2**, and so on. Five is the most. |
| Root | Insight Extractor | Tap the Why that is the real reason, then press **This Why is the root cause**. |
| Who to ask | Lead Interviewer | Only on a Need-to-check Why. Type the person you would ask. Press **Save who to ask**. |

Stop when one Why is the root. That is often the third or the fourth, not always the fifth. Press **Clear the root mark** if the wrong card was marked.

Other students can read the chain. They do not get the Why box. A proposed Why, if one is waiting, has **Accept** for the Insight Extractor.

The root mark is what fills Insight later. If nobody marks one, that blank stays empty on the pin.

## Pin

**Screen:** Pin 1–3 causes to carry forward.

The Insight Extractor chooses the cause and checks two labels. The Theme Mapper places the pin. One to three pins are allowed.

| Field | Who | How | Starts as |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Which cause | Insight Extractor | Press **Choose this cause** on the rib the team followed. | The cause card on that rib. |
| Obstacle | Insight Extractor | The cause itself, edited if the wording will not sit in a sentence. Press **Save labels**. | The text of that cause. |
| Insight | Insight Extractor | The Why marked as the root. If the box is empty, go back and mark the root, or type that Why here. | The marked root. |
| Place the pin | Theme Mapper | Press **Place pin on the fish** after the labels are saved. | |

Both labels need at least a short phrase before **Save labels** works. The Theme Mapper cannot place a pin until the labels are saved.

Everyone else watches. The fish shows the pin after it is placed.

The teacher plays **One sentence, then three keepers**, then presses **Next**.

## Statement

**Screen:** Write one five-blank problem statement each.

Each student writes one sentence. Writing is silent. The other four blanks arrive filled in.

| Field | Label on the screen | How to fill it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Which pin | Which pin feeds this statement? | Only appears when more than one pin was placed. Pick the pin this sentence is about. |
| Who | Who — the person you followed | Already the person from the power map. Change it only if it is wrong. |
| Need | Need — from the interview, edit this | Edit the interview answer into a human need. This is the blank you shape. |
| Obstacle | Obstacle — the cause you pinned | Already the pinned cause. Change it only if it is wrong. |
| Harm | Harm — the incident in the fish head | Already the incident. Change it only if it is wrong. |
| Insight | Insight — the Why marked as the root cause | Already the marked root. Change it only if it is wrong. |
| Deepen | Open deepen (optional) | Context, emotion, impact, alternatives. Not required. |

**Save draft** keeps the sentence. **Submit** sends it. Submit stays off until every blank has text, Who is a real person rather than "users" or "people", and Need has no solution word.

If the map or the fish changes after you save, **Use new version** refills Who, Obstacle, Harm, and Insight. It keeps your Need.

The room moves to the next screen when all five statements are submitted, or when the teacher presses **Next**.

## Choose 3

**Screen:** Clean five-blank statements — pick the top 3.

The five sentences are on the board. Tap one to work on it. Your move changes with your role.

| Field | Who | How |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Tag | Devil's Advocate | After a sentence is selected: Too broad, Multi-problem, Has solution, Generic Who, Missing carry, or Solution in Need. Press the tag again to clear it. |
| Proposed who, need, obstacle, harm, insight | Insight Extractor | Edit the five blanks and press **Propose fix**. Press **None needed** if the sentences are already sound. |
| Accept fix | The author of that sentence | Press **Accept fix**. Nobody else can accept it. |
| Vote | Every student | Two votes. The buttons appear after the Leader opens the vote. The button is labelled with that sentence's Need. |

The Theme Mapper can stack two sentences that say the same thing by tapping one, then the other.

The teacher presses **Next** to lock the pack. If the vote has a clear top three, those three are kept. If the vote was not opened, or the third place is a tie, the room keeps the first three submitted sentences.

## Pack

**Screen:** C60 complete — your pack is ready.

No new fields. The pack holds the map, the fish, and the three statements.

| Control | Who | What it does |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Download pack | Any student | Saves a text file named with the team. |
| Copy pack text | Any student | Copies the same text, to paste to the teacher. |

## What the teacher does on every screen

The teacher view is the class radar, not a second copy of the student fields.

| Control | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Play for the class | Marks that film as played. Students can then open **Watch the film**. The frame is a placeholder until a video file is added. |
| Start or Next | Opens the next screen. On the incident screen, Next also locks the winning incident into the fish head. On the pin screen, Next locks the fish. On Choose 3, Next locks the three statements. |
| Back | Returns to the previous screen. |
| Start time, Pause, −1 min, +1 min | The clock. The teacher runs it. |
| Reset board | Clears the cards, the fish, and the statements. The room code and the roles stay. |
| Open the five seats | Puts all five students on this computer, so one person can test every role. |
| View board | Shows the student board beside the radar. |

**Read this step** on a student screen starts closed. Open it, then open **Read the notes** on a film. The notes stay closed until then.
